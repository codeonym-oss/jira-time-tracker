# jtt collaborators

List the people who can be assigned in a project, with their current load.

![jtt collaborators](gifs/collaborators.gif)

```console
$ jtt collaborators
$ jtt collaborators -p OPS --since 30
```

The load is the number of issues assigned to each person and updated in the last 90 days (or
`--since` days), by status category: to do, in progress and done. The busiest people come
first, and you are marked "(you)".

The names, emails and account ids are what [`--user`](../guide/how-it-counts.md#whose-work-counts)
accepts in the reports.
